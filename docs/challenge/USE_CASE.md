# Use Case: FlyTLV Travel Line

## Who it is for

People in Israel looking for cheap flights from Tel Aviv (round trip or one way) who prefer a quick
phone call over searching websites.

## The problem

Flight prices from Tel Aviv change all the time. Finding the cheapest deal means a lot of
searching across sites.

## What the caller can do

1. **Find flights:** "Where can I fly cheap?" or "Flights to Larnaca".
2. **Filter:** by destination, maximum price, date, or direct flights only.
3. **Hear prices and destinations:** the 2–3 best deals, with city, price, dates and airline.
4. **Save a deal:** "Save the first one."
5. **Be remembered:** on the next call, they hear "Welcome back, last time you saved
   Larnaca for 64 dollars."
6. **Ask for a person:** the call is transferred to a human.

## Example call

> **Assistant:** Welcome to the FlyTLV Travel Line. I'm an AI assistant. I can find you
> cheap flights from Tel Aviv.
> **Caller:** Something cheap to Cyprus in November.
> **Assistant:** Larnaca, Wizz Air, direct, November 9 to 12, sixty-four dollars.
> Want me to save it?
> **Caller:** Yes.
> **Assistant:** Saved. Next time you call, I'll remind you.

## Where the data comes from

Live from the flytlv.app deals API, so the flights, prices and destinations are real.
