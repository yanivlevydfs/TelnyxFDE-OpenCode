Observability

How I know within a minute that it broke

What is instrumented

  • JSON log line per event, with a duration_ms span per request.
  • MetricsCounter actor: counters and latency; scripts/ops/metrics.py.
  • Platform metrics: telnyx-edge metrics shows 2xx/4xx/5xx and p95.
  • trace_id = conversation id, across webhook, actor and MCP.

Where I look first

  1. telnyx-edge metrics fde-webhook: rising errors or p95 near the timeout.
  2. Logs with outcome != ok: rejected = signature, degraded = KV or actor.
  3. Follow one trace_id into the other two services.

The webhook is the canary: every call hits it first.
